Spirograph Drawer

How It Works

  • R: Radius of the fixed outer ring
  • r: Radius of the rolling inner circle
  • d: Pen distance from inner circle center
  • Type: Inside (hypotrochoid) or outside (epitrochoid)
Shapes:
  • Set d = r for sharp cusps (cycloids)
  • Choose integer ratios R:r (e.g., 180:60) for closed curves
  • Try prime or co-prime pairs for intricate patterns

Features

  • Auto redraw: When enabled, updates the drawing as you change inputs.
  • Overlay mode: Draw new patterns on top of the canvas without clearing.
  • Animate: Interpolate the pen offset from Offset A (d1) to Offset B (d2) over the chosen Duration. Use Stop to cancel.
  • Ping-pong: Animate forward and then backward. Combine with Repeat to loop continuously.
  • Color & width: Pick a stroke color and line thickness for the curve.
  • Viewport fit: The canvas auto-resizes to fit the screen without scrollbars; resizing the window triggers a redraw.
